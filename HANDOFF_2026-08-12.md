## Product direction now established

Numberdroid is a **family arithmetic adventure**, not a worksheet level selector.

Every player profile plays the same story/campaign. Arithmetic is personalized, but story order, locations, recognizable robot population, bosses and campaign mechanics remain shared.

Internal production planning currently targets about **25 decks**, roughly grouped into acts, but the player must not see a 25-level roadmap or total act count. The campaign should reveal itself through the current thematic hub.

### Fullscreen boundary

For the browser prototype, fullscreen is requested before intro/title so the player is not interrupted later. The manual fullscreen button remains available for now.

Likely production direction is Capacitor/native shell. If that removes the browser fullscreen issue, remove the startup fullscreen workaround and toggle rather than treating them as permanent design.

### Hub archive screens

Collection, achievements, logbook and statistics are **dedicated full-screen views** opened from the hub. Do not shrink them back into a small side-console panel. They are structural placeholders for systems that will grow.

## Player profiles

A true first install may have zero profiles.

Profile creation:
1. child or adult,
2. name,
3. child gets friendly arithmetic starting estimate,
4. adult currently skips the child knowledge question and gets a higher +/- default.

Each profile independently owns:
- campaign progress,
- current/running mission,
- profile-specific floor save,
- mathematics starting baseline/evidence direction,
- tactical challenge,
- future collection/achievement/story/statistics data.

The latest playtest explicitly confirmed that the child profile and adult profile currently feel well balanced.

## Mathematics / difficulty model

Do not collapse difficulty into one scalar.

Current dimensions:
1. player mathematics baseline/evidence,
2. authored robot math role (`comfort`, `core`, `stretch`, `specialist`, `boss`),
3. local deck curve (easier start → stronger encounters → boss),
4. campaign mechanic complexity,
5. independent tactical challenge (`ENTDECKER`, `STANDARD`, `HERAUSFORDERUNG`).

Easy arithmetic remains valuable even for adults/advanced children. Later decks contain a higher ceiling and more advanced robot roles but should keep some easy/comfort encounters.

Current real arithmetic protocols are addition/subtraction. Do not advertise multiplication/division as playable until actual duel mechanics exist.

Correctness must remain hidden until explicit `REAKTOR AUSLÖSEN`.

## Duel rules that remain binding

- shared 6×5 board
- directed orthogonal chain, bends allowed
- no repeated tile
- minimum 2 numbers
- correctness hidden until submit
- correct length 2 → +1 reactor
- length 3 → +2
- length 4 → +4
- length 5+ → instant normal-opponent win
- wrong submit → board unchanged, lose one reactor segment
- AI is child-friendly and may fail/pass
- collapse/refill animation 560 ms
- MAGNETAR row shift right once/duel
- meta-energy is separate; 1 energy changes a tile ±1

### Boss initiative rule — new binding rule

**The player starts every newly entered duel phase by default.**

For KRONOS this means:
- player starts Firewall 1,
- after Firewall 1 breaks, player starts Firewall 2,
- after Firewall 2 breaks and the core is exposed, player starts the final core phase.

Future exception: an enemy/body trait such as **`REAKTIONSSCHNELL` / Quick Reaction** may explicitly grant the opponent initiative and make that opponent start a duel or new phase. This is future authored ability content; do not silently let ordinary enemies steal first turn.

## Duel loss / HP rule — new binding rule

A lost duel now resets the **whole current deck run** to its authored beginning while applying 1 HP damage.

```text
lose duel
→ damageTaken +1
→ reset current floor state
→ restart same deck at authored start
```

This intentionally resets deck-local progress such as defeated encounters, position/body, acquired deck-local access/resources/actions. It is a small but meaningful penalty.

When the final HP is lost in campaign mode:
- mission fails,
- running mission is cleared,
- player returns to hub,
- next mission attempt starts fresh with restored HP.

Direct developer floor previews may retain the legacy destroyed/restart screen.
